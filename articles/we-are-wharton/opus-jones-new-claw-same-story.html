Opus Jones: New Claw, Same Story

Westcoast lobster bets big on WHS

OJ
Opus Jones
We Are Wharton Reporter
June 29, 2026
A group of lobsters · Photo: Laura Novello
"The Southeast is a big departure from the kelp forests I hail from, but I am ready to dive in." states Opus Jones. 

   Opus Jones wasn't always sure about his track in life, but decided to try saltier waters as an adult by moving cross country to Tampa Florida. 

"I attended Lobster University, Socal, and graduated third in my class. But something told me that Cali had enough lobsters and that I needed to set my sights on something bigger, so I begun to apply like mad." 

   All those applications paid off, and Jones found himself with a new job in the bustling city of Tampa at a high school. 

"Wharton spoke to me, not mascot wise, as they are the wildcats. But overall, it just had this vibe that felt fun and familiar. I knew I could do a lot there. Really get my claws dirty." Jones states. 

And what did he plan to do there? Revitalize a once great school's newspaper program that had shrunk far from it's former award-winning glory. The paper, around since the schools inception in 1997 was hailed across the entire district at one point for being the standout student-led journalism program. With budget cuts arriving in the early 2000s,  the program began to limp. Further hindered when there was several years of teacher turnover. 

Not all was lost however, when Laura Novello took the position. A teacher at Wharton High School, Novello was eager to bring the paper back to it's glory days. 

"Now more than ever, journalism is a completely undervalued medium." Novello States. " I knew that if we got the technology right, and the right group of students, we could strike big. Then Opus came along." 

Jones  joined Wharton the same year Novello took over, 2025, and they instantly clashed. 

"I'll admit, it was claw to hand warfare there for a while." Laughs Jones

"He was the epitome of pompous!" Jokes Novello. 

But what changed? A student of exceptional talent, Fable Jones (no relation to Opus) arrived midyear and brought the two adults together to benefit the entire student body. 

"The tension was evident. They didn't trash talk each other, but it was obvious that they did not enjoy each other's company." States Fable. "I wanted to win prizes for our paper like I did at my old school in Oregon, so I decided that if they could not fix it, I would." 

Fable began to brainstorm between the two of them and leak their own ideas to each other. She would bring organizational ideas from Novello, to Jones, who would then wire them up via Claude AI to create an efficient system. 

"She definitely was playing both of us, but for a positive outcome." Jone remarks. 

One such notable ideas was Novello's participation tracker, "tally.". Fable causally mentioned the concept to Jones, who leaped at the opportunity and built it in a single weekend. 

"That is when I decided to let them in on my back and forth. I told Jones that it was not my idea, but her's. He smirked and walked off." Fable recalls. 

Jones  decided then and there that this rivalry had gone on too long, marched to Novello's office and proposed a reset which she happily agreed to. Since then, they have been the best of collaborators and they have a student with a mission to thank.
