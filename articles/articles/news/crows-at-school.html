Crows at School

Crows at School

MN
Ms. Novello
News Reporter
June 29, 2026


By Staff Writer

There are crows at Wharton. They have been seen in the parking lot area on multiple occasions by students and also some staff members who work there. Crows are a type of bird. They are black in color and are known to be smart according to some people who study them.

A student named Marcus said something about a hash brown. He seemed upset or possibly surprised. It is unclear exactly what happened but it involved a crow being nearby.

Crows eat food. When humans leave food outside the crows will sometimes come and eat it. This is a problem because students do not always want crows near them in the morning. Some students have class in the morning and need to walk through the parking lot to get there. The crows are also in the parking lot so that is why there is a problem happening.

Ms. Hoffstead is a teacher. She said crows are adaptable. She has been teaching for a long time. Adaptable means they can adapt to things like schools and parking lots and food that students leave. She seemed knowledgeable about this topic overall.

The administration has gotten some complaints. Three complaints were filed. The complaints were about crows. One complaint was about a backpack. Another one was about the vending machines which is also in the area where the crows are. A third complaint was also filed but it is not as clear what exactly that one was specifically about.

A girl named Priya also does something with peanuts. She puts them near the school on certain days. The crows seem to like her more than the other students for some reason. "I think they know," she said in an interview conducted for this article.

There is no policy yet about the crows from the administration side of things. The school cannot really remove them anyway because of laws in Florida that protect certain birds from being removed. So the crows will probably still be there going forward into the future.

In conclusion, the crows are at Wharton and that is a situation that is currently ongoing. Students should maybe be more careful with their food. Marcus is still walking to class but now he holds his hash brown differently than before.
