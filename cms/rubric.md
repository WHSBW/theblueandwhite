# The Blue & White — Article Grading Rubric (v1)
*Paul R. Wharton High School student newspaper*

**100 points · six criteria · five tiers each.** Tiers are the same meaning
everywhere: **Exemplary 100% · Proficient 75% · Developing 50% · Beginning 25% ·
Not present 0%.** A criterion's points = its tier % × its weight, rounded to the
nearest half-point.

This rubric does triple duty: it tells the **student** what's expected, tells
the **AI second-opinion grader** how to score, and tells the **adviser** what
she's confirming. The adviser confirms every score before it counts — the AI is
a second opinion, never the final word.

**Not graded by this rubric** (handled by the platform, not the AI):
- **Word count (550–600):** the CMS already computes it; short/over is flagged
  automatically, no AI involved.
- **Process steps** (interview proof, photos, peer edit, editor review, social
  to-go): tracked by the platform's existing features, not graded here.

**News vs Editorial:** same rubric. For **Editorial**, the Interview / Source
criterion is graded on **3+ integrated research sources** instead of an
interview. The article type is passed to the grader so it applies the right
version of that one criterion.

---

## 1. Lead — 15 pts
*The hook plus the key info, up front.*
- **Exemplary (15):** Sharp opening that hooks AND delivers the core news (who / what / why) in the first sentence or two. Reader knows the story instantly.
- **Proficient (11):** Clear, functional lead; main point lands but the hook is mild or the key info is slightly delayed.
- **Developing (7.5):** States a topic but is flat or generic; buries the main point, or hooks without informing.
- **Beginning (3.5):** Weak or confusing opening; reader unsure what the story is; reads like a textbook or starts mid-thought.
- **Not present (0):** No identifiable lead, or the opening is unrelated to the article.

## 2. Structure / Inverted Pyramid — 20 pts
*Most newsworthy info first, details descending logically.*
- **Exemplary (20):** Textbook inverted pyramid — most newsworthy info first, details descend logically, nothing important buried.
- **Proficient (15):** Generally follows the pyramid; minor ordering issues or one buried detail.
- **Developing (10):** Some structure, but key info shows up late; feels out of order or padded.
- **Beginning (5):** Little discernible structure; rambling or chronological; reader hunts for the point.
- **Not present (0):** Disorganized to the point of incoherence.

## 3. Angle & News Value — 15 pts
*Answers "why should I care?"*
- **Exemplary (15):** Clear, compelling angle that answers "why should I care?"; gives the topic purpose and relevance.
- **Proficient (11):** Identifiable angle; relevance present but could be sharper.
- **Developing (7.5):** Topic covered, but the angle is vague ("this is about X"); little sense of why it matters now.
- **Beginning (3.5):** Barely an angle; reads as a flat summary with no point.
- **Not present (0):** No angle; purposeless or off-topic.

## 4. Clarity & Accessibility — 15 pts
*A casual reader with zero background can follow it.*
- **Exemplary (15):** A casual reader with zero background understands fully; jargon defined; smooth sentences.
- **Proficient (11):** Mostly clear; an occasional unexplained term or clunky sentence.
- **Developing (7.5):** Frequently unclear; assumes background; jargon unexplained; reader has to work.
- **Beginning (3.5):** Hard to follow throughout.
- **Not present (0):** Incomprehensible.

## 5. Interview / Source Integration — 20 pts
*News: interview woven in. Editorial: 3+ research sources integrated.*
- **Exemplary (20):** Quotes / ideas woven in smoothly and advance the story; clear attribution. *(Editorial: 3+ credible sources integrated to support the argument.)*
- **Proficient (15):** Quotes / sources relevant but a bit dropped-in or under-explained; attribution mostly clear.
- **Developing (10):** Minimal or awkward use; feels tacked on; weak attribution.
- **Beginning (5):** A quote / source appears but is irrelevant, unattributed, or misused.
- **Not present (0):** No interview material or sources at all.

## 6. Mechanics & Style — 15 pts
*Grammar, spelling, consistent newspaper style.*
- **Exemplary (15):** Clean grammar / spelling; consistent newspaper style; tight, active prose.
- **Proficient (11):** A few minor errors; style mostly consistent; doesn't impede reading.
- **Developing (7.5):** Several errors; inconsistent style; some sentences need rework.
- **Beginning (3.5):** Frequent errors that distract or impede meaning.
- **Not present (0):** Riddled with errors; unreadable.

---

**Total: 100 points.** The AI returns a tier + a short justification per
criterion; the adviser reviews and confirms before anything is recorded.
